## 3. L1 Analysis

### Positioning

NVIDIA should be treated less as a pure chip vendor and more as an AI infrastructure platform company. The L1 evidence supports that framing because the company describes its business around accelerated computing and AI infrastructure, while FY2026 revenue scale shows data center demand has become the central business driver.

### Why It Matters Now

NVIDIA sits at the bottleneck of AI infrastructure spend. For meeting prep, the highest-value follow-up questions are not only product capability, but supply continuity, export compliance, customer concentration, and whether AI factory demand remains durable.

## 4. Insight

事实：FY2026 revenue reached 215.9B USD, and NVIDIA frames AI factories as the next AI infrastructure wave.

推理：Revenue scale and narrative both point to data center AI demand as the primary company-level driver.

结论：NVIDIA's company profile should be anchored on AI infrastructure, not only GPU hardware.

影响：BD, investor, or competitive prep should focus on ecosystem lock-in, supply chain resilience, regulatory exposure, and customer concentration rather than only product benchmarks.

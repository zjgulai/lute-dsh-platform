## 一、九风格设计库（原则 + 模板）

**通用后缀（建议每条提示词末尾追加）**

`Original logo design, unique symbol and lettering, must not resemble any existing trademark or famous brand mark, commercially safe, clean background suitable for cutout.`

### 1. Modern Minimal（现代极简）

- **原则**：少即是多；几何单一焦点；字标定制感；高小尺寸可读性。
- **模板**：`Modern minimalist logo for "{BRAND}", flat vector, simple geometric mark, generous whitespace, refined custom wordmark, no gradients or 3D, single primary color {PRIMARY_COLOR}, subtle neutral gray optional, Swiss-inspired grid alignment, professional DTC brand identity.`

### 2. Abstract Geometric（抽象几何）

- **原则**：抽象符号可多重解读；模块重复与节奏；避免具象抄袭知名图标。
- **模板**：`Abstract geometric logo for "{BRAND}", constructed from basic shapes (circles, arcs, rectangles), harmonious proportions, symbolic but non-literal, paired with clean sans wordmark, limited palette, contemporary corporate friendly.`

### 3. Retro American（复古美式）

- **原则**：徽章/盾形/带状排版；复古衬线或板衬线；做旧适度，保留印刷清晰度。
- **模板**：`Retro American vintage badge logo for "{BRAND}", classic emblem layout, bold slab or vintage serif typography, subtle distress texture optional, heritage color palette (navy, cream, deep red accents), works on labels and packaging, not copying any existing beer or sports team marks.`

### 4. Hand-Drawn Vintage（手绘复古）

- **原则**：手绘感与可复现性平衡；线条有机但不脏乱；适合手工艺、温暖母婴调性。
- **模板**：`Hand-drawn vintage logo for "{BRAND}", organic ink or brushstroke illustration integrated with lettering, warm earth tones, artisanal maternal care aesthetic, high resolution linework, avoid childish clipart look.`

### 5. Fashion/Beauty Light Luxury（时尚美妆轻奢华）

- **原则**：细线、大留白、柔和金属或哑光金；Editorial、轻奢而非堆砌钻石。
- **模板**：`Fashion and beauty inspired light luxury logo for "{BRAND}", elegant thin strokes, sophisticated serif or high-contrast sans, soft rose gold or champagne metallic accent, plenty of negative space, premium nursing wear and maternal wellness brand mood.`

### 6. Modern Tech/Futuristic（现代科技/未来感）

- **原则**：精准几何、微光点缀克制；适合智能母婴硬件子线，避免廉价霓虹堆叠。
- **模板**：`Modern tech logo for "{BRAND}", precise geometry, subtle futuristic accent (thin luminous line or micro gradient), dark or light mode variants implied, trustworthy medical-adjacent cleanliness, not cyberpunk cliché overload.`

### 7. Cute Cartoon Premium（可爱卡通高级童趣）

- **原则**：圆角、柔和配色、吉祥物简化；**高级**在于比例与留白，避免低幼粗糙描边。
- **模板**：`Premium cute cartoon mascot logo for "{BRAND}", friendly simplified character harmonized with rounded wordmark, soft pastel palette, scalable vector style, maternal and baby brand appropriate, not copying existing cartoon IPs.`

### 8. Solid Bold Geometric（实心粗体几何）

- **原则**：强识别、远距可读；色块填充为主；适合促销物料与主图缩略图。
- **模板**：`Solid bold geometric logo for "{BRAND}", heavy filled shapes, strong silhouette, high contrast, simple wordmark with sturdy letterforms, optimized for small app icon and thumbnail visibility.`

### 9. 3D Luxury Premium（3D 奢华高级）

- **原则**：体积感克制（避免过度渲染）；金属/釉面层次与品牌色结合；母婴高端线默认优选。
- **模板**：`Premium 3D luxury logo for "{BRAND}", refined dimensional bevel, elegant metallic material (rose gold or champagne gold), soft studio lighting, subtle shadow on base plane, couture-level restraint, maternal premium sub-brand, not jewelry brand knockoff.`

## 二、金属材料参考（3D/轻奢类）

| 材质关键词（英文提示用） | 效果与适用 |
|--------------------------|------------|
| Brushed aluminum / satin metal | 科技、洁净、中性高端 |
| Polished rose gold / champagne gold | 女性向、礼盒、Pro 线 |
| Pearl ceramic / glazed enamel | 温和、母婴亲和力、非冰冷金属 |
| Matte copper accents | 复古奢华点缀，控制用量 |
| Soft chrome highlights | 未来感硬件子品牌，避免镜面过强 |

**原则**：同一 Logo 主材质**不超过两种**金属语义，避免杂乱；母婴场景优先**柔和反射**而非镜面高光。

## 四、背景标准

- **主标初次输出**：纯色或极轻渐变背景（白、极浅灰、浅米色），便于抠图与反白测试。
- **规格板**：统一浅灰网格或品牌色条带分区，**不抢主标**。
- **Mockup**：场景虚化或统一模板，保证 Logo 区域对比度充足。
- **禁用**：与主标色相邻接的杂色复杂纹理（除非用户明确要求场景稿）。

## 六、设计原则与优化优先级

1. **识别优先**：货架缩略图与头像尺寸下，核心形状仍可辨认。
2. **信任优先于花哨**：母婴相关避免过度暗黑、尖锐刺痛联想（除非品牌明确叛逆定位）。
3. **延展优先**：横版、方版、单色、刺绣简化路径提前考虑。
4. **一致性**：同一任务内所有衍生资产追踪**同一主标**；修改主标后须重做衍生。
5. **优化顺序建议**：可读性 → 独特性（合法范围内）→ 美感 → 场景氛围。
